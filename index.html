Our History

In 1996, Jon Bucklin left an investment advisory role at a large national bank to start Wealth Management Partners. He wanted to move away from transactional bank and brokerage models and build a practice centered on advice, sound planning concepts, and lower fees.

In 2023, WMP Insurance Services and WMP Retirement Plan Services were formed and combined with Wealth Management Partners to create WMP Financial Group.

Today, Dan O'Toole Sr. continues that legacy as owner and principal, preserving the planning-first roots behind the WMP name.

Our Process

Simple steps. No pressure. Your decision.

WMP keeps the first conversations straightforward, respectful of your time, and clear about what working together would look like.

01

Initial Call

In 10-15 minutes, we explain how we work and help you decide if a deeper conversation makes sense.

02

Discovery Meeting

In 30-45 minutes, we listen, ask questions, and discuss how planning and implementation could support your situation.

03

Your Decision

You take your time. If you choose to move forward, we schedule a formal planning meeting and begin the engagement.
