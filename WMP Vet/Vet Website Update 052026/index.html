About VFG

A planning-first practice, narrowed to a profession we know.

Why veterinarians only

Veterinary Financial Group was created because veterinary medicine sits in a financial space all its own — high education debt, modest early-career income, owner economics that don't follow a standard small-business template, and a consolidating market that puts seven-figure decisions in front of practice owners with little warning.

Generalist advisors miss this context. We don't. By limiting our practice to veterinarians, every conversation begins with the assumptions that already apply: PSLF eligibility, hospital buy-in math, corporate buyer playbooks, associate contract structure, relief 1099 cash flow, and the realities of retiring out of clinical practice.

We're independent, fiduciary, and fee-based — built to give advice that isn't shaped by a product to sell.
